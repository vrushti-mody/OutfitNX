THE OUTFIT NX POINT OF VIEW

ROOTED IN SION

Good style feels
like you.

At Outfit NX, fashion is personal. Here in Sion, Mumbai, we bring Indian ethnic wear, Indo-Western fusion and Western clothing together under one roof—so there is room to dress for every side of yourself.

We believe the right outfit starts with how it feels: the fabric against your skin, the way it moves, the confidence it gives you. Our team is here to help you browse, compare and find a look that fits your occasion and your own sense of style.

Drop by, take your time, and discover the collection in person at Satyam Building, Sion Circle.
