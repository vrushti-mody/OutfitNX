Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains how Outfit NX (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit this informational website. Our store is at Shop No 9, Satyam Building, Sion Circle, Sion, Mumbai 400022, Maharashtra, India. For privacy questions, visit the store or call +91 90043 71387.

This website currently has no customer account, contact form, checkout, or first-party analytics. Do not send sensitive personal information to us through public social media.

Information this website uses

We do not ask you to submit personal details on this website. The site stores your cookie choice (“Accept” or “Essential only”) in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your preference. That preference stays on your device and is not sent to Outfit NX. Standard hosting and security logs may be processed by the website hosting provider, such as IP address, browser details, requested pages and timestamps, for delivery, security and troubleshooting. Our hosting provider processes these logs to maintain the availability and security of the website.

Maps, fonts and external services

The interactive Google Map is not loaded until you select “Load interactive map.” If you choose to load it, your browser connects to Google and may send technical data; Google may use cookies or similar technologies under its own terms and privacy policy. A separate directions link also opens Google Maps when selected. The site requests typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive technical request data such as your IP address. We link to Instagram, which has its own privacy practices. We do not control these external services. You can avoid the map by using the address or calling the store.

Why we use information

Any limited technical data available to the hosting provider is used to deliver and secure the website, diagnose errors, and prevent abuse. We do not use this website to profile visitors, sell personal data, run targeted advertising, or collect analytics. If these features are added later, this policy and the consent controls will be updated before activation.

Cookies and your choice

The cookie notice offers “Accept” and “Essential only.” At present, the site does not deploy optional first-party analytics or advertising cookies, so both choices only store your preference in local storage. The interactive map only connects to Google after you request it, and external services opened by you may use their own technologies under their policies. You can clear local storage and cookies in your browser settings.

Retention and security

We keep the cookie choice in your browser until you clear it or replace the choice. Our hosting provider retains technical logs in line with its operational and security practices. We use HTTPS in production and limit collection to what is needed for this simple website. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and provisions as they come into force, you may have rights to request information about personal data, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. To make a request or raise a concern, contact the store by phone or in person using the details above. We will verify the request as appropriate and respond within applicable legal timelines. For privacy questions or requests, call us or visit the store using the contact details above.

Children

This website is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Please do not submit a child’s personal information through external platforms linked from this site.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when site features, service providers, or applicable requirements change. The latest date appears at the top of the page. Outfit NX, Shop No 9, Satyam Building, Sion Circle, Sion, Mumbai 400022, Maharashtra, India. Phone: +91 90043 71387.
